# Audio

## Music
To match the intense, high speed strategy of the gameplay, the music will have a high BPM during fights. When the game is paused themusic will slow down and transition into the pause theme. A similar transition will happen with losses. The music slows down before gracefully cutting to a modified main theme. During sections between fights, the music will be considerably slower with reverb to create a sense of calm and relive some tension.

## SFX
The sound effects wil have a 'crunchy' feel, like a classic arcade game. This will immerse the player further into the retro style of the game furthering the desired player experience.

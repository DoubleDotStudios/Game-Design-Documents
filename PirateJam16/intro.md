# Introduction

## Game Summary Pitch
[name] is a topdown, autoscrolling boss rush where you are the dungeon. You control the booby traps to eliminate the monsters that block the path of a brave adventurer who wishes to take win treasure.

## Inspiration

## Player Experience
For [n] rooms you must strategically use your traps to defeat the bosses. Each of your traps has a cooldown so be strategic about what you use at what time. Each boss has a unique set of attacks so be ready for anything. I your adventurer is to perish then you'll have to start again and learn from your mistakes.

## Platform
The game is developed primarily for Web.

## Development Software
- Godot 4.3:   Programming
- Libresprite: Assets + GUI
- Bosca Ceoil: Music
- Jsfxr:       Sfx

## Genre
- Boss rush
- Strategy

## Target Audience
With simple elements of planning but fast paced gameplay, the game is geared for those who enjoy the Boss Rush genre but want a new challenge. And, for strategy players who like a more offensive side to their ideas.
